Featured case study

Bus E-Ticket Booking System

A digital ticketing solution designed for one bus company to reduce queues at physical booking offices and simplify booking operations.

RoleStudent Developer / System Analyst
FocusWeb, database, booking & payments
SDLCSpiral development
StatusIn development

Problem

Travellers may have to queue at a physical booking office to reserve seats. Manual booking also creates additional work for operators when managing reservations, payments and ticket records.

Proposed solution

The system provides an online flow where a traveller selects a route and trip, chooses an available seat, provides required passenger details, completes mobile payment, and receives an electronic ticket/receipt through email.

Core users

  • Travellers — search trips, book seats and receive e-tickets.
  • Bus operator/admin — manage routes, trips, seats, bookings and ticket records.
  • System — validates booking information, records transactions and generates tickets.

Planned core modules

Trip & route management

Routes, schedules, fares and trip availability.

Seat booking

Seat availability and reservation management.

Payment

Mobile payment integration and transaction status.

E-ticket

Ticket generation and email delivery.

Administration

Booking records, reports and operational controls.

Security

Authentication, validation, access control and audit considerations.

Development approach

The project is being developed using the SDLC with an iterative Spiral approach: identify objectives and risks, design and prototype, develop and test, gather feedback, and refine the next iteration.

Next milestone: complete the system requirements and database design, then move into the next implementation iteration.
