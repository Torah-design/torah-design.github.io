Project

Healthcare Appointment System

A system concept focused on making healthcare appointment scheduling more organized for patients and service providers.

Purpose

The system is intended to reduce unnecessary waiting and improve appointment coordination by providing a structured digital process for requesting, scheduling and managing appointments.

Potential modules

Patient registration

Capture and manage appropriate patient account information.

Appointment scheduling

View available appointment slots and make requests.

Provider management

Manage healthcare providers and their schedules.

Notifications

Appointment confirmations and reminders.

Skills applied

System analysis, requirements modelling, database design, web development and information systems security.
